Use cases

Designed Around Real Student Challenges

These are illustrative example scenarios — not customer testimonials.

Example scenario

The undecided sophomore

Challenge: Has to declare a major soon and doesn't know what different majors actually lead to.

How Smart Mentor helps: Compare career directions and required skills across majors, try a short hands-on challenge from each, and bring better questions to an academic advisor.

Example scenario

The working student

Challenge: Works 25 hours a week and only has small pockets of time for anything beyond coursework.

How Smart Mentor helps: A 30-minute daily plan that balances deadlines with one small skill-building step, so progress still happens on busy days.

Example scenario

The internship hunter

Challenge: Has good grades but nothing concrete to show on a resume or talk about in interviews.

How Smart Mentor helps: Turn completed challenges into portfolio projects, format them on a resume, and practice answers that reference real work.
