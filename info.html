About

Smart Mentor is an early-stage platform for college students in the United States. Our mission is to help students succeed in college, discover their career direction, build practical skills through daily hands-on activities, and prepare for internships and jobs — all in one personalized workspace.

How it works today

Smart Mentor is in its early version. Your workspace (tasks, goals, projects, resume, and practice answers) is saved on your own device. Personalized plans and practice feedback use built-in guidance rules; AI-powered features are on our roadmap.

What Smart Mentor is not

Smart Mentor supports your planning and practice. It does not replace academic advisors, qualified career professionals, counselors, or instructors, and it does not guarantee admission, grades, or employment outcomes.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your first name and email address when you create an account or log in.
  • Onboarding answers: education level, major, academic year, career direction, daily time available, and main focus.
  • Plan upgrades: your name, email, and plan when you upgrade. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card number.

We use this information to run and improve Smart Mentor and to contact you about your account. We do not sell your information.

Information that stays on your device

Your password (stored only as a one-way hash), tasks, goals, portfolio projects, resume content, and interview answers are saved in your browser's storage on your device and are not sent to us. You can erase them anytime from Settings → Reset my workspace, or by clearing your browser's site data.

Third-party resources

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and icons from a public content delivery network (unpkg). Those providers may receive standard request information such as your IP address and browser type.
